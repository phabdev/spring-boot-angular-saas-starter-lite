# Launch readiness

€49 early access, €99 planned standard and a possible €199 agency license are proposed prices supplied by the product owner. No demand or revenue evidence is available. A functional build is insufficient evidence of a sellable product.

Before activating paid checkout, the owner must provide:

- A merchant checkout URL, reliable private delivery of the Pro archive and a purchase receipt flow.
- Final seller identity/contact details, license/customer terms, privacy and refund policy consistent with the actual sale.
- A support channel and a defined update policy with realistic capacity.
- A verified release artifact, independent review and results from target developers installing/extending it.
- A waitlist provider and privacy information if lead collection is enabled.

The landing fails closed when commerce configuration is absent. It explains the unavailable state and does not claim that a purchase or subscription occurred. Repository publication, email collection, ad spend and checkout activation have not been performed merely by building the site.

Run the bounded validation described in marketing/README.md. Track qualified developers who successfully install and extend the kit, concrete objections, and willingness to pay; clicks alone do not validate the offer.
